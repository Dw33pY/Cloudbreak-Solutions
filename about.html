About Cloudbreak Solutions

More than protecting paper.

Cloudbreak Solutions is a cybersecurity and document-security company specialising in the protection, authentication and verification of high-value digital and physical documents.

How we work Talk to our team

WHO WE ARE

In an increasingly interconnected world, the integrity and authenticity of information have become critical to organisations, institutions and individuals alike. Cloudbreak Solutions develops and implements sophisticated security technologies designed to combat counterfeiting, forgery, tampering, unauthorised duplication and document fraud.

Our solutions integrate multiple layers of security into a single document — incorporating more than ten advanced security features that strengthen document integrity while enabling efficient, reliable authentication. By combining secure design, specialised printing technologies, authentication features and verification systems, we create documents that are significantly more difficult to reproduce, manipulate or counterfeit.

"Document security is more than protecting paper — it is about protecting identity, information, reputation and trust."

Our expertise extends across certificates, credentials, official documents, identification materials and other high-value documentation — wherever authenticity, traceability and security are essential. Through continuous innovation and the disciplined application of security technology, we empower organisations to issue documents with confidence — and to verify them with greater certainty.

[ 03 ] HOW WE WORK

Three layers. One document.

Physical, visual and digital security are designed together — because a document's protection should be a system, not a list of parts.

01
PHYSICAL

It starts with the sheet.

Security paper with unique fibres and textures; true-tone watermarks formed during manufacture; hard, waterproof stock; invisible fibres woven through the pulp. Before a single drop of ink, the substrate is already fighting back.

02
VISUAL

Then the print.

Guilloché lattices, microtext, rainbow split-duct graduation, thermochromic and optically variable inks, foils, embossing and anticopy structures — each one chosen to be checked by a different pair of eyes, from a registrar to a border officer.

03
DIGITAL

Finally, the record.

Unique serials — sequential, randomised or encrypted — plus barcodes, QR codes and signed data that tie the physical document to a verifiable source of truth. The paper can be checked; the record can't be argued with.
