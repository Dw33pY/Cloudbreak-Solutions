[ 05 ] WHY IT MATTERS

A missing layer is an unpriced risk.

Failing to secure documents doesn't save money — it defers a loss. The two columns below are the same organisation, two different decisions.

What strong security delivers

  • Protecting documents — features safeguard them from forgery, alteration, duplication and unauthorised reproduction.
  • Protecting information — secure design prevents unauthorised access, manipulation or misuse of confidential data.
  • Building trust & authenticity — the public can rely on official documents being genuine, and verify it.
  • Supporting compliance — measures that help meet requirements for issuing and protecting official documents.

The cost of a missing layer

  • Counterfeiting & forgery — documents reproduced or manipulated for fraudulent purposes.
  • Unauthorised duplication — sensitive documents copied or distributed without permission.
  • Document tampering — critical information altered, removed or replaced without detection.
  • Fraud & financial loss — fake documents facilitate fraud and significant financial damage.
  • Loss of trust & reputation — document fraud undermines public confidence and organisational credibility.
